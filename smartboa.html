SMARTBOA

Design and development of an advanced underwater marine detection device aimed at environmental monitoring.

The SMARTBOA project integrates mechanical design with sensing technologies to create a reliable floating buoy system. The work covered the entire product development phase, from 3D CAD modeling and fluid dynamics considerations to the integration of the internal measurement hardware.

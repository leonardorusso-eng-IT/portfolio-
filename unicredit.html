UniCredit - Startup Your Life

Selected for the prestigious "Startup Your Life" educational program promoted by UniCredit, I served as Team Leader in the conceptualization and development of a startup project based on an innovative payment method. This experience bridged the gap between engineering and business, requiring us to navigate the entire entrepreneurial lifecycle—from initial ideation and feasibility studies to organizational structuring. The program involved hands-on workshops on business planning, pitching, and financial management, significantly strengthening my decision-making and problem-solving skills in a fast-paced, non-technical environment.

Key Learnings

  • Skills: Business Strategy, Financial Planning, Team Leadership, Pitching
  • Focus: Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Organizational Feasibility

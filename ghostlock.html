Ghostlock - EDTH Hackathon

Winning 1st Place Overall at the European Defense Tech Hackathon, GhostLock is an edge-native autonomous Counter-UAS interceptor drone tailored for GPS-denied environments. As Team Leader and Mechatronics Engineer, I co-developed the closed-loop perception-to-pursuit logic integrating an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and a Pixhawk 6C flight controller. By fine-tuning YOLO models and implementing Global Motion Compensation (GMC), we reduced the edge-detection compute load by 89% using an adaptive region-of-interest strategy. Additionally, I implemented a 3D localization pipeline via visual triangulation and Kalman filtering, achieving a 2.72m mean tracking deviation without reliance on ground stations.

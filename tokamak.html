Tokamak Reactor Mirror Optimization

This project presents the thermo-mechanical modeling and active deformation mitigation of the ECRH front-steering mirror (M2) for the Divertor Tokamak Test (DTT) machine. Operating under extreme high-power microwave heat loads, the mirror experiences significant out-of-plane thermal gradients. Through Multi-Physics FEM investigations in Ansys, I analyzed the resulting membrane stresses and deflection. To counteract the "dimple effect" singularity caused by centralized point-pulling, I designed a bio-inspired spider-web structural backing to uniformly distribute the load. The study culminated in the simulation of piezoelectric (PZT) actuators arranged in a bridge mechanism to actively flatten the mirror surface, validated through a Rayleigh-Ritz analytical 2D formulation.

Tools and Technologies

  • Simulation & Analysis: Ansys Mechanical, Multi-Physics FEM, MATLAB
  • Key Concepts: Thermo-elasticity, PZT Actuation, Rayleigh-Ritz Method

DYNAMIS PRC - Formula Student

As a Structural Engineer for the DYNAMIS PRC Formula Student Team (2022-2026), I managed the custom design and optimization of brake calipers and uprights for our racing prototypes. Through rigorous FEM dynamic analysis and thermal simulations, I achieved a 30% mass reduction and a 25% increase in stiffness for the braking system compared to commercial alternatives, perfectly optimizing the brake line performance via MATLAB scripts. Furthermore, I led the R&D development of the uprights, utilizing topology optimization and additive manufacturing studies to yield an additional 20% mass reduction while increasing structural stiffness by 10%.

Tools and Technologies

  • Modeling & Simulation: SolidWorks, ANSYS Mechanical, Abaqus, Ntopology
  • Programming: MATLAB

Deep Learning Anomaly Detection

Applied deep learning project focused on identifying manufacturing defects using unsupervised computer vision models.

Utilizing the standard MVTec AD dataset, I developed and trained models capable of recognizing structural and textural anomalies on industrial components without prior labeling of the defects. The project demonstrates the potential of AI in automating quality control processes.

Tools and Technologies

  • Software & Frameworks: Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow
  • Key Concepts: Unsupervised Learning, Computer Vision, Quality Control
